# Supplied Christmas, ornament, and Mini Marie photos

Twenty-one figures from six user-supplied clipboard lineups: Christmas 2016
secret Reindeer and Gingerbread Robby; four 2022 ornaments; six 2014 ornaments;
four 2008 samples; Mini Marie-Antoinette secret; and four Francfranc 2007 figures.
Originals are retained under ignored deliverables, with per-file provenance
in `sources.json` and crops in `scripts/photo_batches/christmas-ornament-mixed.json`.

The ornament display-ball inset is excluded; individual 2014 figures are used.
The supplied Mini Marie has no ornament loop and maps to the non-ornament
Ladurée collection. Francfranc pink Santa/Reindeer are secrets; metallic
gold/brown figures are regular. The 2008 group explicitly displays as
Christmas Series 2008 Sample in tracker and stock.

Local u2net masks preserve original RGB/print texture without generation
or upscaling. Existing IDs and collection data are retained.
