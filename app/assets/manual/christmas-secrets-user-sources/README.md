# Christmas supplied secret photos

Eighteen supplied figures across 2021, 2020, 2019, 2018, 2017, 2012, and 2011.
The 2017 Rocking Horse is regular; the other seventeen are secrets.
Distinct secret Santa/Reindeer designs are mapped to secret IDs, not the
regular counterparts. Robby bases, the 2021 cup handle, antlers, and pendant
designs are preserved. All existing IDs and collection records are retained.

Sources are seven user-supplied clipboard images, preserved as year-named
PNGs under ignored `deliverables/christmas-secrets-cutouts/source-photos/`.
Local u2net masks preserve source RGB and print texture without generation
or upscaling. Reproducible settings: `scripts/photo_batches/christmas-secrets.json`.
Individual source provenance is recorded in `sources.json`.
