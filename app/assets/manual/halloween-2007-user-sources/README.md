# Halloween Series 2007 supplied photo cutouts

Four transparent PNGs from one supplied lineup: regular Pumpkin and Ghost,
plus the gold secret Pumpkin and Ghost. Linked to their four existing catalog
IDs, preserving existing secret flags and collection data.

Local background removal retains source RGB and the soft original 500x500
lineup resolution. The white Ghost silhouette and pale hands are restored with
traced alpha contours revealing original photo pixels. No figure details were
generated. Alpha corrections are recorded in
`scripts/photo_batches/halloween-2007.json`; original filename, crop boxes and
output dimensions are recorded in `sources.json`.
