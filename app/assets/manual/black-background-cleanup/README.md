# Flat black background cleanup

23 existing catalog images repaired locally without changing their figure artwork, names, or IDs. Removes near-black regions connected to the border and visually verified enclosed background regions selected by explicit seeds. Source RGB and foreground alpha remain intact, including eyes and dark costume details. No model inference or image generation.

Original copies remain in deliverables/black-background-cleanup/source-photos. Manifest scripts/photo_batches/black-background-cleanup.json records original catalog asset paths, threshold, and enclosed-region seed coordinates. sources.json records provenance. Includes six collaboration figures, Morning/Sunset Glow, three Cloud figures, six Santa’s Little Helper 2025 figures, and six Pumpkin Patch 2025 figures.
