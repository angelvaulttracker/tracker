# Circus and collaboration supplied photos

27 figures: Long Legged Clown Robby (2022), Silk Hat Rabbit and Clown Robby
(original Circus), WAAC 2023 and 2022 (four each), H Family (six), Dashin
Socks (five), and MODI 2015 (five). Sources and crop coordinates are recorded
in `scripts/photo_batches/circus-collaboration.json`; originals are retained
under ignored deliverables.

The old Long Legged Clown asset depicted Merry-go-round; the old original
Clown Robby asset depicted Silk Hat Rabbit. Supplied distinct figure photos
replace both mistaken assignments. MODI 2015 white Rabbit is regular and
blue Rabbit is secret. H Family Saiun-Nominami and Scarecrow are secrets.
Existing IDs and saved collection records are retained.

Local u2net masks preserve original RGB/print texture without generation
or upscaling. Clown Robby's ball, Silk Hat Rabbit's hat, and Scarecrow base
are retained. Per-asset provenance is in `sources.json`.
