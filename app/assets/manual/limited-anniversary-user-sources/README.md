# Limited and anniversary user photographs

80 transparent cutouts from 21 user-supplied guidebook photographs. Local cached u2net processing preserves original RGB without generated artwork. Original photos are saved in deliverables/limited-anniversary-cutouts/source-photos. Crop boxes and any mask corrections are recorded in scripts/photo_batches/limited-anniversaries.json and sources.json.

Includes Lucky Meow (4), Black Pineapple Love You (3), 100Pt Crown (3), Chinese Festival (4), Ishigaki Pineapple (1), two Shisa variants, Seoul Lucky Bags (2), Mango Rabbit (1), both pink Dragon variants, ten miscellaneous Limited models, Cafe Series (12), anniversary decoration and photo contest figures (4), Birthday Party Special Color (2), Follow Your Heart (3), 19th/18th/17th Anniversary (3), 15th Anniversary Robbys (4), Colorful Cake Rabbit secret (1), full 11th Anniversary (14), 12th Anniversary secrets (2), and Guide Book exclusives (3).

Catalog IDs remain unchanged. Guide Book IDs have historical misleading slugs: page-80-17-kiss-mark-leopard-rabbit is the Robby; page-80-16-rabbit is the heart Rabbit; page-80-15-robby-angel is the Kiss Mark Leopard Rabbit. Assignments follow the verified catalog names and supplied photo. Shisa and birthday color variants retain their distinct IDs.
