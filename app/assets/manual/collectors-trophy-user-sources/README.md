# Collector's Trophy supplied photos

Thirteen trophies from supplied clipboard images
`682be764-f589-49b0-9876-bc8d08357815` and
`80a3ff04-0566-4561-b94a-82435b464a15`. Originals are preserved in ignored
`deliverables/collectors-trophy-cutouts/source-photos/`.

Original pedestals are retained. Collector and Authorized Dealer pink
rabbits remain distinct, as do Lucky Cat L and S sizes. The Rabbit Red
entry on page 86 was not supplied and retains its existing artwork.

Local u2net masks preserve original RGB and print texture without
generation, recoloring, or upscaling. Existing catalog IDs are preserved.
Reproducible crops and alpha corrections are recorded in
`scripts/photo_batches/collectors-trophy.json`; provenance is in `sources.json`.
