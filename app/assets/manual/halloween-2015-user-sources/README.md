# Halloween Series 2015 supplied photo cutouts

Six transparent PNGs: Werewolf, Pumpkin Cat, regular Vampire, regular Skeleton,
secret green Vampire and secret green Skeleton. Extracted locally from two
user-supplied images, retaining original RGB and source resolution. The regular
lineup is only 350x350 pixels, so those four cutouts have limited detail. No
figure pixels were generated.

The green Skeleton crop excludes the floor grid beneath its feet. The reusable
batch is `scripts/photo_batches/halloween-2015.json`; source filenames, crop boxes
and dimensions are recorded in `sources.json`.

Existing catalog IDs are preserved. The regular Vampire was previously marked
secret; its explicit flag is corrected to false. The green Vampire and Skeleton
are explicitly marked secret. Skeleton Robby Angel was not supplied and remains
unchanged. These assets do not complete the Robby image gap for this series.
