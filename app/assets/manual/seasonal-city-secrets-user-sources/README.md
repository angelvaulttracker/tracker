# Seasonal and city-series user photographs

42 transparent cutouts made locally from user-supplied photos with cached u2net weights. Source RGB is preserved; no generated artwork. Sources, crop coordinates, and manual alpha corrections are recorded in sources.json and scripts/photo_batches/seasonal-city-secrets.json.

Includes Kiss me Robby, Easter 2017 secret bunny, eight Summer Caribbean Sea 2016 figures, Happy New Year 2023 and 2021 (Rabbit/Cow photo), Circus 2022 Ringmaster/Horse, Town Musicians, UFO, Wonderland, Dog Time Lucky and Secret figures, Enjoy the Moment, Classical, Happy Little Bear, Japanese Good Luck, NewYork, Seoul, and Okinawa. Catalog IDs remain unchanged. Seven Lucky figures retain Lucky badges separately from Secret labels. The Summer sunglasses accessory inset has no separate catalog entry and is not mapped.
